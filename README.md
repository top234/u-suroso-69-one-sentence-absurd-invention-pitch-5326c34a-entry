## The pitch

> The Doorbell Butler answers the door for you, signs for your parcel in a tiny shaky hand, and slides it under the doormat before the neighbours can steal it.

A doorbell that does the whole door-answering job itself — so you never have to get up for a parcel again, and the parcel never sits outside long enough to vanish.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies) writes the pitch to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), validating first that it is a single non-empty line of 1–220 characters.
